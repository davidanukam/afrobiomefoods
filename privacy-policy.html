Privacy Policy

App: Igbo Food Heritage (Android package: com.afrobiomefoods.igbofoodheritage)
Developer: Afro Biome Foods
Effective date: May 23, 2026
Last updated: May 23, 2026

Afro Biome Foods (“we,” “us,” or “our”) operates the Igbo Food Heritage mobile application (the “App”). This Privacy Policy explains what information the App collects, how we use it, whom we share it with, and the choices you have. We wrote this policy to meet Google Play’s transparency requirements and to describe how we handle sensitive user and device data.

By using the App, you agree to this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

1. Summary

  • You can browse recipes, events, and services without creating an account (guest mode).
  • We collect account and community-post data only when you choose to sign in and post content.
  • We do not sell your personal information, run in-app advertising, or use third-party analytics or crash-reporting SDKs in the App.
  • We do not collect your precise device location. Maps show fixed service locations from our catalog.
  • Most accessibility and language preferences stay on your device.

2. Information we collect

2.1 Information you provide

  • Account registration (optional): If you sign in with email and password, we collect your email address and authentication credentials. Passwords are processed and stored by our authentication provider; we do not store plain-text passwords on your device or in our own servers.
  • Google Sign-In (optional): If you choose “Continue with Google,” Google authenticates you and may share basic profile information (such as your name and email address) with us through our authentication service. Google’s use of your data is governed by Google’s Privacy Policy.
  • Community posts (optional): If you are signed in and submit a story or memory, we collect the text you enter, a display name (derived from your profile name or email), your account identifier, language preference, post type, and audience setting (e.g., community or family-only label). Posts you submit are stored on our servers.

2.2 Information stored on your device

The App stores the following locally on your phone or tablet (using secure on-device storage), and this data is not uploaded unless noted:

  • Language preference (English, Igbo, or French)
  • Accessibility settings (text size, high contrast, audio guidance toggle)
  • Onboarding and guest-mode status
  • Authentication session tokens when you are signed in (so you stay logged in)

2.3 Information collected automatically

  • Content downloads: When our backend is configured, the App requests recipes, events, services, and community posts from our database host. Standard connection metadata (such as IP address and request timestamps) may be logged by that provider as part of normal service operation.
  • Realtime updates: The App may subscribe to live updates for catalog and community content through our database provider’s realtime channel when enabled.

We do not intentionally collect:

  • Precise or coarse GPS location from your device
  • Contacts, photos, microphone recordings, or calendar data (voice recording is planned for a future release and is not active in the current version)
  • Advertising identifiers for targeted advertising
  • Health, financial, or government ID data

2.4 Device features you control

These features process data on your device or only when you take an action:

  • Text-to-speech: When you use “listen” or cooking audio guidance, the App uses your device’s speech engine to read recipe text. That processing is handled by your operating system; we do not receive recordings of your voice from this feature.
  • Haptic feedback: Optional vibration for button presses, processed locally.
  • Phone calls: If you tap to call a listed service, the App opens your device’s dialer with a phone number you chose to call. We do not monitor the call.
  • Share sheet: If you share a recipe, you use your device’s native share dialog; we do not receive what you send or to whom.
  • Maps: The services map displays predefined locations from our catalog. It does not track where you are. On Android, map tiles may be provided by Google Maps as part of the map component; Google may receive technical data needed to render maps according to its policies.

3. How we use information

We use information to:

  • Provide recipe, nutrition, event, and service directory features
  • Authenticate you and maintain your session when you sign in
  • Publish and display community posts you submit
  • Operate admin tools for authorized staff who manage catalog content
  • Remember your language and accessibility preferences
  • Protect the App against abuse and enforce our content rules
  • Comply with law and respond to lawful requests

We do not use your information for targeted advertising or to build advertising profiles.

4. How community and public content works

Recipes, events, services, and community posts stored in our database are readable by anyone using the App (subject to our access rules). When you post to the community wall while signed in, your display name and post text may be visible to other users. Choose what you share carefully and do not post sensitive personal information you do not want others to see.

Audience labels (such as “family only”) are stored with your post for organizational purposes; they do not encrypt or hide content from our administrators or from the technical design of the public read policy. Do not rely on the App for private medical, legal, or financial communications.

5. Legal bases (EEA/UK users)

If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, we process personal data on these bases:

  • Contract / service: To provide features you request (e.g., account login, posting).
  • Legitimate interests: To operate, secure, and improve the App, and to display community content, balanced against your rights.
  • Consent: Where required (e.g., optional sign-in or future features such as voice recording).
  • Legal obligation: When we must comply with applicable law.

7. Data retention

  • Account data: Kept while your account is active. You may request deletion (see Section 9).
  • Community posts: Kept until you delete them (while signed in) or until your account is deleted, after which they are removed with your account where our database rules apply.
  • On-device settings: Remain on your device until you clear app data or uninstall the App.
  • Server logs: Retained by our providers according to their default periods, then deleted or aggregated.

8. Security

We use industry-standard measures including encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS) to our backend, authenticated access controls, and row-level security rules in our database so users can manage only their own community posts. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure; we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • Access, correct, or delete personal data we hold about you
  • Export your data in a portable format
  • Object to or restrict certain processing
  • Withdraw consent where processing is consent-based
  • Lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority

In the App:

  • Use the App as a guest without signing in.
  • Sign out from Settings to end your session on the device.
  • Delete community posts you created while signed in (where the App exposes delete controls).
  • Clear local data by clearing storage for the App in Android Settings or uninstalling the App.

Account deletion: To delete your account and associated community posts, contact us at igbolondon.canada@gmail.com. We will verify your request and complete deletion within a reasonable period, subject to legal retention requirements.

California residents may have additional rights under the CCPA/CPRA (know, delete, correct, opt out of sale/share). We do not sell personal information as defined by California law.

10. Children’s privacy

The App is intended for a general audience interested in Igbo food heritage and culture. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or under 16 in the EEA without parental consent). If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

11. International users

If you use the App from outside the country where our servers are located, your information may be transferred to and processed in other countries, including the United States, which may have different data protection laws than your home country. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards for such transfers.

12. Permissions (Android)

The App is designed to work with minimal permissions. Features you use may indirectly involve:

  • Internet: to load remote recipes, events, services, community posts, and sign-in.
  • Network state: to detect connectivity (via the operating system or libraries).
  • Vibrate: optional haptic feedback.

We do not request access to your location, camera, microphone, contacts, or SMS in the current App version. Future features (such as voice stories) will be accompanied by updated disclosures and permission prompts before use.

13. Google Play Data safety alignment

For Google Play’s Data safety form, the App may collect or process:

  • Email address: optional, for account creation and sign-in
  • Name: optional display name from Google profile or derived from email
  • User IDs: account identifier assigned by our auth provider
  • Other user-generated content: community post text you submit
  • App interactions: limited to content you create (posts); we do not use analytics SDKs to track in-app behavior

Data is encrypted in transit. Authentication tokens are stored on-device. Data is not sold.
