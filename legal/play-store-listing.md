<b>Igbo Food Heritage</b> brings traditional Igbo cooking, nutrition, and culture to your phone. With thoughtful accessibility for every generation.

Whether you are reconnecting with home, teaching a child a family dish, or exploring Nigerian cuisine for the first time, the app helps you cook with confidence: clear ingredients, step-by-step guidance, and the stories behind each meal.

<br /><br />

<b>◉ Recipe library</b><br />
▪ Browse soups, porridges, delicacies, snacks, and pepper soups<br />
▪ Ingredients, instructions, health notes, and cultural context<br />
▪ Search and filter by category<br />
▪ Share recipe summaries with friends and family<br />

<br />

<b>◉ Audio cooking mode</b><br />
▪ Hands-free step-by-step guidance while you cook<br />
▪ Listen in <b>English</b>, <b>Igbo</b>, or <b>French</b> using built-in text-to-speech<br />
▪ Replay steps at your own pace<br />

<br />

<b>◉ Built for accessibility</b><br />
▪ Adjustable text size (standard, large, extra large)<br />
▪ High contrast mode for easier reading<br />
▪ Optional audio navigation hints<br />
▪ Large touch targets and readable layouts<br />

<br />

<b>◉ Community & memory</b><br />
▪ Read food memories and stories on the community wall<br />
▪ Share your own written stories when you choose to sign in<br />
▪ Celebrate Igbo food heritage across the diaspora<br />

<br />

<b>◉ Events & local services</b><br />

▪ Find community kitchens, markets, and cultural centers on a map or list<br />

<br />

<b>◉ Nutrition & healthy aging</b><br />

<br />

<b>◉ Your language, your pace</b><br />
Switch the interface and recipe content between <b>English</b>, <b>Igbo</b>, and <b>French</b>. Continue as a guest to explore recipes, or create an account with email or Google when you want to post to the community.
